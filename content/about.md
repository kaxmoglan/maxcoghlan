I'm a technical lead in FinTech, based in Winchester. Day to day I'm
responsible for a group of engineers and what they ship — not just the code,
but how the work gets scoped, how it's estimated, how it reaches production,
and how the people doing it are growing.

I came up as a full-stack developer — TypeScript and React on the front end,
Node, GraphQL and Postgres behind it — and I still read and write code most
weeks. But the job now is judgment: deciding what's worth building, keeping a
team pointed the same way, and being the person clients and stakeholders can
talk to directly about technical trade-offs.
