1. **Understand** — Talk to the people closest to the problem before touching
   the backlog.
2. **Align** — One team, one reason why. Decisions get faster once that's
   settled.
3. **Ship** — In pieces small enough to learn from, not big enough to fear.
4. **Grow** — People outlast any one project. That's what I optimise for.

## One-liner version

For designs that only have room for a sentence:

> The best technical decision is usually the one that lets the team move
> faster next quarter.

## If we drop the numbered framing

Some of the designs don't suit a list. A prose alternative:

> The same loop every time: understand the real problem before committing to
> a plan, get the team aligned on why, ship in pieces small enough to learn
> from, and spend as much effort on the people as on the product.
